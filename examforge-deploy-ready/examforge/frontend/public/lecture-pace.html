Import from a YouTube playlist

Easiest: open the playlist, scroll to the bottom so every video loads, press Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C and paste into the box above. Titles and lengths are picked out for you.

Cleaner option, and it also brings the video links: on the playlist page open the browser console, paste this line and press Enter, then paste the result above. Some browsers ask you to type "allow pasting" first, and YouTube changes its page often, so this may need a tweak.

Playlists that list the newest video first come in upside down. The reverse option above fixes that, and it is also applied for you when titles count down, like Lecture 45 first.
